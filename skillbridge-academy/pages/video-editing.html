Video Editing Course with AI for Beginners

This 3-month video editing course teaches you to edit reels, ads and long YouTube videos from scratch. You learn timeline editing, audio mixing, colour correction, titles and basic motion graphics in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, then speed up your workflow with AI tools for captions, voiceovers and background removal. You finish with a 60-second brand reel and a 3-minute YouTube video for your portfolio.

⏱ 3 months Beginner to Advance 6 modules

Course roadmap

Complete the modules in this order.

1

Video Editing Basics: Timeline, Cuts and Storytelling

Timeline, cuts, pacing, shot types, storytelling

2

Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve Essentials

Project setup, trimming, transitions, export

3

Audio Editing: Noise Removal, Mixing and Voiceovers

Noise removal, mixing, voiceovers, sound design

4

Colour Correction, Grading and Motion Titles

Colour correction, grading, motion titles

5

Motion Graphics Basics for Editors

Keyframes, masks, simple animation, templates

6

AI Video Editing Workflow: Captions, Voice and Repurposing

Auto captions, AI voice, background removal, content repurposing

Final project: Brand Reel and YouTube Video

Edit a 60-second brand reel and a 3-minute YouTube video, using AI tools for captions and audio. Upload both to YouTube or Instagram as your portfolio.
